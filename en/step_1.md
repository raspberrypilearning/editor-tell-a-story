## Add the opening line

Change the heading and first caption so your webpage starts telling your own story.

```html filename="index.html" line_numbers="true" line_number_start="9" line_highlights="9,13"
  <h1>Space adventure</h1> <!-- Change the page heading to your story title -->

  <div>
    <img src="sun.png">
    <p>There was once a star.</p> <!-- Add the opening line of your story -->
  </div>
```

## Now run your code

Click **Run** and check that your page shows your new title and the first line of your story.
